Nutrition for ferrets

What should I feed my ferret?
- Balanced commercial ferret diets are highly recommended for ferret. The ideal diet ratio shall be 30-35% in protein, 15-30% fat and low in fiber. The protein source is meat-based, rather than grains. Meat and their by-products should be the top ingredient of any ferret food.
- An adult ferret needs around 43g/kg dry food and 200-300 calories/kg per day. 


How oftern should I feed my ferret?
It's best to leave food out so they can have their meals 24 hours a day.Because ferrtets have a very short gut transit time.

Can I change my ferrets' diet frequently?
- Do not change ferrets' diet frequently. Most ferrets enjoys one brand of commercial diet for their entire lifes. Changing diet can lead to upset stomach, diarrhea and pancreatic problems.


How often should I feed if my ferret develop low blood sugar?
Ferrets having hypoglycemia(low blood sugar) should be fed several small meals during the day.

What should I avoid in my ferrets' diet/treats?
Avoid feeding sweets, dairy products, raisins, fruits, and vegetables to your ferrets. These food may cause diarrhea. Do not feed chocolate, as it can be fatal to ferrets.

How much water does my ferret need during a day? 
Make sure clean water is available 24 hours per day. Most ferrets will perfer a heavy bowl over a sipper bottle. Change the water daily to prevent bacteria and yeast growing.
