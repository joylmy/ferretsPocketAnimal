What Else Should You Keep in Mind?
1. Block all openings (holes) that lead to areas you cannot reach. Ferrets can squeeze through surprisingly small openings.
2. To prevent ferrets from burrowing into the foam inside couches, chairs, and mattresses, block access to these areas with thin plywood or hardware cloth.
3. Keep foam, plastic, and rubber objects like earplugs, erasers, and headphones out of ferrets' reach. Swallowing these items can completely block the intestines, which can be fatal.
4. Older ferrets should be caged separately since they sleep more.
5. Clean litter boxes and remove uneaten food daily.
6. Clean the cage with soap and water at least weekly, and rinse well.
