What others shall I pay attention to
1. Block all openings (holes) that lead to areas you cannot reach. Ferrets can squeeze through surprisingly small openings.  
2.To preven ferrets from burrowing into the foam inside couches, chairs and mattresses, block access to these areas with thin plywood or hardware cloth.
3.Keep foam, plastic, and rubber objects like shoe inserts, earplugs, erasers, headphones out of your ferret’s reach.  Swallowing these items can cause a life-threatening intestinal blockage. 
4. Older ferrets should be caged seperately since they sleep more.
5. Clean litter boxes and remove uneaten food daily.
6. Clean the cage with soap and water at least weekly, and rinse well.
